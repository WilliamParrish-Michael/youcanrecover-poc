How it works

Compassionate help, one step at a time

We're a marketing & consulting team that connects people to care. No pressure, no judgment — just a clear path forward.

1. Reach out

Call, text, or send a message. Tell us what you or your loved one is struggling with — we listen without judgment.

2. We match you

We assess the right level of care and connect you with trusted, vetted programs that fit your needs, insurance, and location.

3. Start care

We help coordinate admission and stay in your corner — so getting into treatment feels supported, not overwhelming.

Levels of care

The right support for where you are

Recovery isn't one-size-fits-all. We connect you with the full continuum of care across the United States.

Detox

Detox & residential

Medically-supervised detox and 24-hour residential treatment for those who need a safe, structured start.

PHP

Partial hospitalization

Full-day clinical programming with the flexibility to return home or to sober living in the evening.

IOP

Intensive outpatient

Structured group and individual therapy that fits around work, school, and family responsibilities.

Housing

Sober living homes

Safe, supportive, substance-free housing that helps new routines and community take root.
