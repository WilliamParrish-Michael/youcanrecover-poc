Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

YouCanRecover is committed to making our website usable for everyone, including people who use assistive technologies. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Use semantic, structured HTML so screen readers can navigate the page.
  • Provide sufficient color contrast and readable, resizable text.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Ensure the site remains readable and usable even without JavaScript.
  • Respect the "reduce motion" setting for animations.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We continue to test and improve, and some third-party content may not yet fully conform.

Need this information another way, or found a barrier?

If any part of this site is difficult to use, or you need help or information in a different format, please contact us — we'll do our best to help promptly.
