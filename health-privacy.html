Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains how [Company Name] LLC (“[Company Name],” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects consumer health data. It supplements our Privacy Policy and is provided to meet consumer-health-data laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada SB 370, and Connecticut’s health-data provisions. Where this policy and the general Privacy Policy differ regarding health data, this policy controls.

We are a referral and consulting service, not a medical provider or a HIPAA-covered entity. Please share only what’s needed for us to help connect you with care — you don’t need to send us detailed medical records.

What we mean by “consumer health data”

Information that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. For us, that primarily means what you tell us about a substance-use or recovery situation (for yourself or a loved one) when you contact us, together with the contact details you provide in connection with that request.

What we collect and why

  • The details you share about the substance-use/recovery situation, and your name, phone, and email — collected so we can understand your needs and help connect you with appropriate care.
  • We do not knowingly collect precise geolocation to identify health facilities, and we do not use geofencing around treatment or healthcare locations.

Consent to collect

We collect your consumer health data only with your consent — for example, the consent you give when you check the box and submit our contact form. You may decline; you can also choose not to provide health-related details and still ask a general question.

How we share it — and your authorization

  • Treatment providers. To fulfill your request, and with your authorization, we share your contact information and a summary of your request with vetted treatment providers so they can reach you about care.
  • Service providers. Vendors that help us operate (for example, our form-delivery provider, which relays your submission to us by email) act on our instructions and may not use your health data for their own purposes.
  • We do not sell your consumer health data. We will not sell it, and will not share it for anyone’s third-party marketing, without your separate, valid written authorization.
  • Legal & safety. When required by law or to protect someone’s safety.

Your rights

You may ask us to: confirm whether we hold your consumer health data and access it; obtain a list of who we shared it with; delete it; and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawal doesn’t affect processing already carried out). To exercise any of these, email hello@example.com or call (555) 123-4567. We will respond within the timeframe required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response.

Retention & security

We keep consumer health data only as long as needed to help connect you with care or as required by law, then delete or de-identify it. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to consumers whose consumer health data we process, and expressly extends the protections of the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, and Connecticut law to residents of those states. Residents elsewhere may also have rights under their state’s privacy law (see our Privacy Policy).

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to reach our privacy contact: email hello@example.com or call (555) 123-4567.
